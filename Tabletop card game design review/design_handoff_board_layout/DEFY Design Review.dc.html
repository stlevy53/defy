Design review · DEFY! v0.1.9

The board doesn't fit on the board.

Two problems in this UI are the same problem. Cards are too small to read because the play area is capped at 1260px on a 1920px screen, and you scroll because everything that got squeezed out of the width went into height. Fix the spatial model and readability comes along with it. Below: a faithful recreation of the current PLAN screen with the findings marked on it, then the same game state rebuilt to fit one screen, then the ATTACK state showing the feedback changes.
